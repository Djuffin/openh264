## 2. `fork-shared(S63)` — the category and its soundness argument, once

**The shape.** The encoder's multi-threaded mode forks with `std::thread::scope`
(`slice_multi_threading.rs`, three scope blocks) and hands every worker the *same*
`sWelsEncCtx`. N workers cannot each hold `&mut` to one allocation — that is not a
style constraint but the definition of a data race under the aliasing model — so
every function body reachable from inside the fork keeps its `*mut sWelsEncCtx`
parameter (and the cursors derived from it) permanently, for as long as the fork
shares one context. F66 proved the converse in Phase 6 (a `&mut ctx` function-entry
retag invalidates the whole context; session J of that phase made the conversion,
Miri refused it, it was reverted), and S63 names the rule.

**Why it is sound.** Within the fork: reads of shared context state are reads of
fork-constant data (parameters, tables, geometry) or go through the two ruled seams
— `recon-seam` (D-mt-3: one `UnsafeCell`-crossing accessor + one `unsafe impl Sync`,
with Miri data-race probes) and the atomics F132's nine contention rounds installed
(`pOverallMbMap`, `iSliceNumInFrame`, …). Writes are either slice-disjoint by
construction (each worker's slice range) or atomic. The referee is not this
argument but the two **multi-threaded fork/join Miri probes** — the full encode
under the aliasing checker, green since T9.E8 and re-proven at every close and at
this exit — plus the 583-configuration byte sweeps in both profiles.

**Membership.** A body is in the category iff `phase9_forksplit.py` reaches it
from the fork seeds (109 of the 111 ctx-param bodies at this close, plus the
in-fork layer/SMB/MD/deblocking/CABAC surface and the old `MT` tags, which named
the same seam before the theorem had a name). 204 tags at this close. The tag is
lawful *while the fork shares one context*; D-perf-6-class redesigns (per-worker
context splits) would re-open it, which is exactly why the category names its
premise.

## 3. The other lawful categories

- **`instrument(test)`** (55) — tags on `#[test]` items: the S28/S40 cursor
  round-trip tests, kernel parity tests, and Miri probes' helpers. They are the
  instrument, not the debt; each retires with the surface it tests.
- **`lawful-single(F166)`** (1) — `ParasetStrategy`: the unbound-lifetime accessor
  whose raw form is what makes ten sound-by-allocation call sites legal (flipping
  it is E0499 at every one).

  layer-parameter retag is what invalidated `InitDqLayers`'s live pointer; the

  hand out is stored into `SLayerBSInfo::pBsBuf` (`codec_app_def.h:640`), which
  crosses the C boundary and can never carry a lifetime.

  the feature-search kernels (installed only under `bScreenContent`,
  `svc_motion_estimate.rs:541`), the lossless-ref-selection preprocess surface,

  `WelsMdInterFinePartitionVaaOnScreen` (one of the two ST-flippable ctx bodies —

## 4. The queue — what stays tagged, and who owns it

**293 tags stay** (`port-raw(Phase 9)` 290 + `cursor` 3), in four kinds:

1. **ST callers of fork-kept accessors (180)** — bodies outside the fork
   whose only unsafety is calling the raw accessors the fork keeps. NOT locked by
   S63: H3 proved the remedy (a safe sibling accessor per family, then per-body

   marked in the appendix):

   - `default`: 24 bodies
   - `current_layer`: 13 bodies
   - `ctx_pps_array`: 9 bodies

   - `slice_in_bank`: 6 bodies
   - `WelsEncodeNal`: 4 bodies
   - `ctx_sps_array`: 3 bodies
   - `ctx_func_list`: 2 bodies

2. **The never-owned ST surface (60)** — the charter's `other` family that no
   session took (its §6.1 said so): `au_set.rs` writers with scalar out-params,
   `wels_encoder_ext.rs` validation, `svc_enc_slice_segment.rs` slice-argument
   init, `WelsSetMem*` fills, the preprocess picture-pool accessors (shape-blocked
   per session X: pool-picture plane views the `Vec` shape cannot express), the VAA
   kernels. Mixed convertible/blocked; each needs its call sites read (F187's rule).

3. **Safe-shaped freebies (37)** — no raw in signature or body, no unsafe
   callees; `unsafe` should lift with the compiler as referee, one pass.

4. **Slot-typed kernels and installers (15)** — `extern "C"` bodies shaped by
   dispatch-slot typedefs (`WelsCopy*_c`, `WelsIDct*Rec_c`, RC method-table
   entries). Their disposition follows the per-slot READ census (exit condition 3):
   a slot never read deletes its kernels; a live slot's typedef decides the shape.

5. **Dead (1)** — `WelsInitSliceEncodingFuncs` (`svc_encode_slice.rs`): empty body,
   zero callers in the port (`grep -rn 'WelsInitSliceEncodingFuncs' src tests benches`
   = the definition and its tag alone) and zero in upstream (`grep -rn` over
   `codec/` = the declaration `svc_encode_slice.h:167`, never defined or called).
   First deletion of the maintenance queue.

6. **The decoder's deblocking slot table (13, added at step 3)** —
   `common/deblocking_common.rs`'s `extern "C"` shims behind `DeblockingInit`.
   They were never tagged because the file never carried a per-file
   `#![deny(unsafe_code)]`, and the crate root had none either until session J
   (**F203**). They are the decoder-side analogue of what session F
   de-virtualized on the encoder side, and they belong to Phase 5's family, not
   Phase 9's — the tag says so at each site. **The queue is therefore 306, not
   293**, and the growth is a hole closing, not work regressing.

**Owner**: maintenance / Phase 10, with this document as the work order. The
ratchet keeps every number from growing; the census regenerates the queue.
